# VYBE 1.9.9.13 — Individual animated palette environments

Replaces the eight recycled background modes with 17 distinct procedural scenes, one for every color palette (including Auto). Ember has flickering flame tongues and rising glowing cinders; Vaporwave a neon sunset and perspective grid; Ocean water caustics; Forest fireflies; Cosmic nebula and stars; Aurora curtains; Golden Hour rays and dust; and each remaining palette has its own movement and composition.

Existing background source toggle, animation speed, performance-mode motion freeze, and darkness control are preserved. Uses the existing ticker and Canvas drawing only. Drive lifecycle remains unchanged.

Source archive only; Flutter compilation and real-device performance require testing.
