# VYBE v1.9.9.51 — Album cover reuse

- Reuse validated artwork for other tracks with the same nonempty artist and album.
- Store confirmed album covers in the existing persistent media cache.
- Search the album cache before requesting online artwork.
- Do not reuse album artwork if artist or album is missing.
- Retains fanart, background blur, and existing UI.

Source patch only. Flutter compilation and Android device behavior have not been verified.
