# VYBE 1.9.9.47 — Background Blur Control

- Visuals > Background: adjustable Background blur slider (0–18).
- Default 6 retains the existing album-art blur.
- Artist fanart stays sharp at the default, as before. Values above 6 add blur to fanart, up to sigma 12.
- Applies to the artwork background under Club Lights / Tunnel combination modes.
- No changes to foreground album artwork or visualizer effects.

Source-level change only. Flutter compilation and device testing not performed.
