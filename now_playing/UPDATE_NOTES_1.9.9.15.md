# VYBE 1.9.9.15 — Club Light Show

New third Background source option: **Club Light Show**. A single integrated, fully choreographed scene (not a collection of independently selectable FX) featuring atmospheric haze, motorized moving-head beams, crossing laser fans, sequenced LED wall, mirrorball reflections, and floor lighting. Uses selected accent palette and existing background animation slider. No hard strobes, additional animation controllers, external assets, or audio permissions. Performance mode freezes the show. Existing album and template backgrounds unchanged. Drive fixes remain paused.

Not compiled in this environment; verify with GitHub Actions and test on ARM32 ATOTO and ARM64 Samsung.
