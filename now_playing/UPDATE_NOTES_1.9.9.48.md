# v1.9.9.48 — Record Cut fanart continuity

When fanart is the selected background and has loaded, the Record Cut large disc is now translucent rather than an opaque grayscale album-cover image. The same full-screen fanart remains visible under the disc in portrait and landscape. The disc keeps a dark tint and edge, while the vinyl and visualizer remain above it. Existing album-cover disc behavior is unchanged when fanart is unavailable or not the active background.

Source-level change only; Flutter SDK is unavailable in this environment, so compilation and on-device rendering remain unverified.
